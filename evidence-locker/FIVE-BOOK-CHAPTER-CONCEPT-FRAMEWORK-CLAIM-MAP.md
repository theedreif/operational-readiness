# Five-Book Chapter → Concept → Framework → Claim Map

## Pass Six — Chapter-Level Intellectual Index

**Series:** *The Asymmetrical Life* five-book corpus  
**Author:** Ed Reif  
**Mapping rule:** manuscript text first; synthesis second.  
**Purpose:** make every major chapter/section addressable as a semantic unit and connect it to the concepts, frameworks and claims it supports.

> **Important:** “Mapped claim” means the concise proposition this index assigns to the chapter from the manuscript’s argument. It is not represented as a verbatim quotation unless separately quoted in the source.

## Canonical chain

```text
BOOK → CHAPTER / SECTION → CONCEPT → FRAMEWORK → CLAIM
                                      ↓
                              CROSS-BOOK DOCTRINE
                                      ↓
                         TOOL / APPLICATION / EVIDENCE
                                      ↓
                                  PROVENANCE
```

## Mapping vocabulary

- **Concept** — the idea the unit primarily develops.
- **Framework** — the named or functional mechanism that makes the concept usable.
- **Mapped claim** — the proposition supported by that unit.
- **Cross-book recurrence** — the same concept/framework appearing elsewhere in the corpus.
- **External theory** — established theory referenced by the manuscripts; it is not converted into Ed Reif IP by inclusion.

## Provenance and IP Boundary

This index distinguishes authored synthesis from referenced intellectual traditions. Concepts such as dual-process theory, bounded rationality, Nash equilibrium, loss aversion, deliberate practice and other established ideas retain their external intellectual lineage. Named manuscript formulations and the relationships synthesized here can be indexed as authored publication material without claiming ownership of those external theories.

Field experiences support the manuscripts where documented. They do not imply ownership, endorsement, employment or client relationships beyond the provenance established elsewhere in this repository.
