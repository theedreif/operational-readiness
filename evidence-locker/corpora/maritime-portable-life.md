## Evidence relationship

This is a **public evidence corpus**, not a claim that every travel story is a separate framework or piece of IP. It provides longitudinal source material for the portable-life, reinvention, mobility, uncertainty, and asymmetrical-life branches of Ed Reif's work.

The archive also acts as a bridge between lived maritime experience and later published doctrine:

**SEA & TRAVEL → ADAPTATION → CONSEQUENCE → OPTIONALITY → DECISION SCIENCE → PORTABLE CAPABILITY**
