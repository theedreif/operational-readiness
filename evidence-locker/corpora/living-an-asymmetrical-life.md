# Living an Asymmetrical Life — Cross-Domain Evidence Corpus

## A life understood as field evidence, not a single-origin story

**Corpus type:** Cross-domain lived-experience archive, authored synthesis, decision-science context  
**Primary relationship:** Experience → observation → synthesis → concept  
**Provenance:** Lived-experience archive + publication + public field evidence

This corpus connects the major lived-experience domains in the Ed Reif Field System without collapsing them into a single claim of professional authority or intellectual ownership.

> **MOVEMENT → INTERRUPTION → ADAPTATION → POSITION → ATTENTION → DECISION → UPDATE**

The purpose is not to claim that travel, poker, a pandemic, Fair Isle, Gibraltar, maritime life, family experience, or professional field environments are themselves Ed Reif intellectual property. The corpus documents how public experience archives and later authored synthesis connect to recurring ideas about uncertainty, optionality, adaptation, attention, consequence, and asymmetric exposure.

## 8. Synthesis

The cross-domain pattern is:

```text
LIVED EXPERIENCE
      ↓
OBSERVATION
      ↓
UNCERTAINTY / CONSTRAINT / CONSEQUENCE
      ↓
ADAPTATION
      ↓
DECISION
      ↓
EVIDENCE
      ↓
UPDATE
      ↓
AUTHORED SYNTHESIS
```

A related asymmetrical formulation is:

> **Accept the field. Design the position. Act. Release the outcome. Update. Begin again.**

The corpus supports the proposition that the later books and frameworks synthesize recurring observations across different environments. It does not establish that every lived event was originally undertaken as a formal experiment or that later doctrine existed in its mature form at the time of the underlying experience.

## 9. Connections to the Field System

This corpus connects primarily to:

- **Asymmetrical Decision Making** — exposure, optionality, process and outcome
- **Portable Life** — mobility, adaptation, base and return
- **Adaptation Under Constraint** — response when available options change
- **Judgment Under Uncertainty** — decisions with incomplete information
- **Attention and Companionship** — observation, relationship and meaning
- **Operational Readiness** — only where supported by separately provenance-labeled professional evidence

---

## 10. Evidence Boundary

Use this corpus to support claims about documented lived experience, longitudinal public archives, recurring themes, and authored synthesis.

Do not use it by itself to infer:
- employment or client relationships;
- endorsement by named organizations;
- ownership of public events, places, technologies, or broader academic fields;
- that later frameworks existed unchanged during earlier experiences;
- universal causal claims from personal experience.

See the [Provenance Standard](../../PROVENANCE.md) and [Claim → Evidence Map](../CLAIM-EVIDENCE-MAP.md).
