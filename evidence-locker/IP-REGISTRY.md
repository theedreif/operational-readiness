## Architecture

**IP → CONCEPT → TOOL → CASE STUDY → EVIDENCE**

The registry is designed to make relationships explicit rather than leave books, videos, frameworks and project evidence as disconnected portfolio items.

## Provenance rule

Published artifacts are linked to their public source. Engagement-based evidence is governed by the repository [Provenance Standard](../PROVENANCE.md). Named organizations describe project context; the status of the relationship should be read from the associated case study or evidence record.

## Expansion queue

Future entries can include additional books, curricula, graphic novels, audio briefings, presentations, articles, named models, diagrams, and public project artifacts as their canonical sources are identified.
