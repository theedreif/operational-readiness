# Judgment at the Edge

## Definition
**Judgment at the Edge** is Ed Reif's decision architecture for the point where procedure, centralized direction, or automation no longer fully resolves what to do next.

It focuses on recognizing a meaningful signal, understanding the changed condition, identifying limits, making a justified decision, and preserving evidence for review.

## Core Architecture
**SIGNAL → CONDITION → LIMIT → DECISION → EVIDENCE**

For Drone as First Responder and human-machine environments:

**NOTICE → INTEGRATE → PRIORITIZE → DECIDE → DEFEND**

## Applied Context
The framework is intended for scenario design, autonomous-system training, DFR learning, field decision support, and after-action review.
