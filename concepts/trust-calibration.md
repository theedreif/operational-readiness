# Trust Calibration

## Definition
**Trust Calibration** is the alignment of human reliance on an automated or autonomous system with what the system can actually do under the conditions that apply.

The objective is neither maximum trust nor minimum trust.

> **The objective is calibrated trust.**

Operators need evidence and mental models that help them recognize when to rely on the system, question it, intervene, escalate, or stop.

## Human-Machine Check
**EXPECTED → OBSERVED → GAP → LIMIT → INTERVENE**
