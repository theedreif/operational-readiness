# Scenario Is the Product

## Definition
**Scenario Is the Product** is the design principle that the primary learning deliverable in high-consequence environments is the decision environment—not the slide deck, LMS module, or reference manual.

Supporting content matters, but it exists to enable performance.

> **Scenario is the product. Friction is the mechanism. Evidence is the output.**

## Applied Context
A useful scenario forces a meaningful decision under realistic constraints and produces observable evidence of judgment.
