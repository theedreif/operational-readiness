# Telemetry Over Testing

## Definition
**Telemetry Over Testing** is Ed Reif's principle that readiness should be evaluated through observable performance evidence wherever practical, rather than relying primarily on declarative knowledge checks.

Relevant evidence can include decision latency, actions taken, escalation points, recovery behavior, communication, use of limits, and other observable traces generated during realistic performance.

The principle does not mean quizzes or knowledge checks have no value. It means the strength of the evidence should match the strength of the readiness claim.
