# Human Judgment Around Autonomy

## Definition
**Human Judgment Around Autonomy** is the principle that increasing autonomy changes rather than eliminates the human decision problem.

As direct control decreases, human work can shift toward mission intent, supervision, anomaly recognition, boundary management, escalation, intervention, and review.

> **Autonomy does not remove judgment. It changes where judgment is required.**

## Applied Context
Ed Reif applies this principle to learning and decision architectures for Drone as First Responder, uncrewed systems, and other human-machine operating environments.
