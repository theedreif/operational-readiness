# Operational Readiness

## Definition
In the Ed Reif Field System, **Operational Readiness** is the evidenced capability of a person or team to execute under realistic operational conditions, including friction, ambiguity, changing information, and communication degradation.

Training completion measures exposure or completion. Operational readiness asks a different question:

> **What evidence says the human can perform when the conditions matter?**

## Applied Context
Ed Reif applies this formulation across high-consequence learning, maritime and autonomous-system training, and scenario-based performance design.

## Core Model
**MISSION → CAPABILITY → CONDITIONS → PERFORMANCE → EVIDENCE → GAP → ACTION**
