Operational readiness begins where conventional training often ends.

Knowing a procedure is not the same as recognizing when conditions have changed. Passing a test is not the same as making a defensible decision under pressure. Completion is not evidence of capability.

This repository explores how people prepare to **notice, decide, act, and adapt when reality departs from the plan**.

## The Operating Problem

Technology changes. The operating problem does not:

**Somebody still has to recognize when reality has departed from the plan—and decide what to do next.**

## Core Doctrine

- **Scenario Is the Product** — design around decisions, not information consumption.
- **Friction Is the Mechanism** — capability develops through ambiguity, pressure, constraints, and competing signals.
- **Telemetry Over Testing** — observe decisions and behavior rather than relying only on knowledge checks.
- **Trust Calibration** — know when to trust automation, question it, and intervene.
- **Evidence Beats Completion** — readiness is demonstrated through performance.
- **The 45-Second Rule** — field learning must remain useful when time, attention, and cognitive bandwidth collapse.
