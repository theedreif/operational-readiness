# Ed Reif — Professional Identity & Authority Record

## Summary

**Ed Reif** is an instructional systems designer, learning architect, author, and decision-science practitioner focused on **operational readiness, judgment under uncertainty, autonomous systems, and field learning**.

His work asks a practical question:

> **What can people actually do when reality departs from the plan?**

Reif's public work connects high-consequence learning design, human-machine judgment, scenario engineering, decision science, maritime experience, and autonomous-system training.

## Field System

**Operational Readiness** — build the capability.

**Judgment at the Edge** — make the decision.

**Decision Science** — design the bet.

**Field Notes** — test the ideas against reality.

> **BUILD → DECIDE → POSITION → TEST → UPDATE**

## Core Principles

- Scenario is the product.
- Friction is the mechanism.
- Telemetry over testing.
- Trust must be calibrated.
- Evidence beats completion.
- If it cannot survive the field, it does not count.
