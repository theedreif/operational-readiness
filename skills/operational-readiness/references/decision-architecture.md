# Decision Architecture

## Primary loop

**SIGNAL → CONDITION → LIMIT → DECISION → EVIDENCE**

### SIGNAL
A cue, anomaly, event, change, or piece of information that deserves attention.

Design question: What must the operator notice?

### CONDITION
The interpreted state of the operating environment.

Design question: What is true now, and how does that change the meaning of the signal?

### LIMIT
A boundary on permissible or effective action. Limits can include authority, policy, safety, time, system capability, resources, uncertainty, or mission requirements.

Design question: What constrains the response?

### DECISION
The action justified by the signal, condition, and limits.

Design question: What must the operator choose, and what alternatives were available?

### EVIDENCE
The observable record that makes performance reviewable and supports learning.

Design question: What would show that the operator noticed the right thing, reasoned appropriately, acted within limits, and updated when necessary?

## DFR loop

For Drone as First Responder applications:

**NOTICE → INTEGRATE → PRIORITIZE → DECIDE → DEFEND**

- NOTICE meaningful signals.
- INTEGRATE multiple information sources.
- PRIORITIZE what matters now.
- DECIDE on justified action.
- DEFEND the reasoning with evidence.

## Relationship to readiness

**READINESS BEFORE THE MOMENT → JUDGMENT IN THE MOMENT → EVIDENCE AFTER THE MOMENT**

The purpose is not to guarantee perfect outcomes. It is to make decision quality more observable, defensible, and improvable under uncertainty.
