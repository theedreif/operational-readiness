# Field Principles

## BUILD → DECIDE → POSITION → TEST → UPDATE

Operational Readiness sits inside the Ed Reif Field System.

### BUILD
Prepare capability before the consequential moment. Use realistic scenarios, purposeful friction, compact field tools, and observable performance.

### DECIDE
Exercise judgment when reality departs from the plan. Identify signals, interpret conditions, respect limits, choose action, and preserve evidence.

### POSITION
Structure exposure under uncertainty. Protect the floor, improve the odds, preserve optionality, and remain positioned for upside.

### TEST
Put doctrine into contact with actual or representative field conditions. Look for mismatches between the designed system and real behavior.

### UPDATE
Use evidence to revise guidance, scenarios, thresholds, tools, and assumptions. Treat doctrine and training as versioned operational systems.

## Five practical commands

**TRAIN FOR FRICTION.**

**SCALE JUDGMENT.**

**QUESTION THE MACHINE.**

**CLOSE THE GAP.**

**COMPLETE THE MISSION.**

## Human-machine principle

The technology changes. The operating problem does not: somebody still has to recognize when reality has departed from the plan and decide what to do next.

## Field-support principle

At the point of performance, prioritize:
- cues over exposition;
- thresholds over background;
- decisions over descriptions;
- actions over abstractions;
- evidence over completion.

## Quality check

A field-ready intervention should answer:
- What changed?
- What matters now?
- What are the limits?
- What decision is required?
- What should happen next?
- What evidence should remain?
