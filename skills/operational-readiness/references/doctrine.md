# Operational Readiness Doctrine

## Purpose

Operational Readiness is a field-learning doctrine for preparing people to perform when conditions change, information is incomplete, time compresses, and consequences attach to decisions.

## Core propositions

### Scenario Is the Product
The scenario is not decoration around instruction. It is the environment in which capability is developed and demonstrated. Start with the decision and operating conditions, then determine what information is required.

### Friction Is the Mechanism
Capability must survive realistic resistance. Useful friction can include ambiguity, incomplete information, time pressure, competing signals, degraded resources, handoffs, uncertainty, and unexpected system behavior. Friction must be relevant to the real task.

### Telemetry Over Testing
A conventional test often measures what a learner can recall. Operational telemetry captures what a person notices, chooses, does, communicates, escalates, and learns during performance.

### Trust Calibration
Modern operators increasingly work with AI, automation, sensors, and autonomous systems. Readiness includes knowing when to trust the machine, when to verify it, when to question it, and when human intervention is required.

### Evidence Beats Completion
Completion is an administrative state. Capability is a performance state. Evidence should show whether the person can recognize the situation, act within limits, make a defensible decision, and adapt.

### The 45-Second Rule
Field support should remain useful when attention and time collapse. If critical guidance cannot be located, understood, and acted on rapidly, redesign it for the point of performance.

## Readiness test

Ask five questions:
1. What must the person notice?
2. What must they understand about current conditions?
3. What limits or boundaries govern action?
4. What decision must they make?
5. What evidence demonstrates that the decision and action were defensible?

## Design principle

**Build for the moment the plan stops working.**
