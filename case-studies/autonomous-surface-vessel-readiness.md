## The situation

A maritime technology company builds unusual craft, including uncrewed platforms. The platforms share hardware, software and an operator interface, but do not inherit a mature training tradition.

The programme required curricula built to a formal defence training standard ahead of customer acceptance trials.

## The problem underneath the requirement

An autonomous surface vessel does not have a conventional helmsman. Someone sets intent — waypoints, parameters, mission envelopes — and supervises a system executing that intent at a distance.

Three design problems follow:
1. Certify judgment rather than throttle handling.
2. Certify one person against multiple role sets without diluting either.
3. Build a curriculum that survives software change.

## Approach

**Dual-hat operator/maintainer architecture.** A layered competency set integrates maintenance, diagnostics, fault rectification, integration and operating qualification.

**Scenario as the certifying artefact.** Assessment is built on scenarios rather than knowledge checks, using SDD-F-O: Situation, Data, Decision, Friction, Outcome.
→ [Scenario Is the Product](../concepts/scenario-is-the-product.md)

**Trust calibration as an explicit outcome.** Operators encounter cases where a system can be confidently wrong, not merely broken.
→ [Trust Calibration](../concepts/trust-calibration.md)

**Version literacy built into the curriculum spine.** Every module asks what changed since the last version and what that changes about failure.
→ [Human Judgment Around Autonomy](../concepts/human-judgment-around-autonomy.md)

**A documentation suite that survives handover.** Analysis traceability, competency mapping and assessment rationale are designed to remain usable after transition.

## What it demonstrates

- Curriculum design to a formal defence training standard
- Competency architecture for small-crew autonomous platforms
- Assessment design that produces evidence of judgment rather than attendance
- Curriculum built to absorb continuous engineering change
