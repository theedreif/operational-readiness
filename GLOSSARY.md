# Ed Reif Field System Glossary

A compact vocabulary for Operational Readiness, Judgment at the Edge, Decision Science, and human-machine field learning.

## Bounded Exposure
Structuring a decision so a credible adverse outcome remains survivable and does not remove the ability to make the next decision.

## Calibrated Trust
Reliance on an automated or autonomous system that is proportionate to its demonstrated capability, current conditions, limits, and available evidence.

## Decision Architecture
The deliberate structure connecting signals, conditions, constraints, choices, consequences, and evidence so judgment can be exercised and reviewed.

## Evidence
Observable information that supports or challenges a claim about capability, judgment, system behavior, or outcome.

## Field Learning
Learning designed around the conditions, decisions, constraints, and performance requirements of the environment in which capability must actually work.

## Friction
A realistic constraint or disruption that forces a learner or operator to adapt rather than merely reproduce a rehearsed answer. Examples include uncertainty, time pressure, degraded communications, conflicting information, system anomalies, and changing priorities.

## Judgment at the Edge
Decision-making where procedure, centralized direction, or automation no longer fully determines the next action. See [Judgment at the Edge](concepts/judgment-at-the-edge.md).

## Operational Readiness
Evidenced capability to execute under the realistic conditions that matter. See [Operational Readiness](concepts/operational-readiness.md).

## Optionality
The preservation of future choices under uncertainty. Optionality is valuable because new information can arrive after an initial decision.

## Scenario
An engineered decision environment that requires a person to notice, interpret, decide, act, and produce evidence under relevant constraints.

## Telemetry
Observable traces of performance generated while work or realistic practice occurs. Depending on the system, this can include decisions, timing, actions, communication, interventions, errors, recoveries, and escalation points.

## Trust Calibration
The process of aligning human reliance on automation with demonstrated system capability and current operating conditions. See [Trust Calibration](concepts/trust-calibration.md).

## Update
A deliberate change to a belief, decision, procedure, scenario, interface, or learning system in response to new evidence.

## Architecture Context

The compact toolkit loop used across several field tools is:

**Toolkit operating loop:** BUILD → DECIDE → POSITION → TEST → UPDATE

It is a local mechanism inside the larger architecture, not the complete Field System.

**Ed Reif Field System:** BUILD → DECIDE → POSITION → COMMUNICATE → PROVE → UPDATE → DEPLOY
