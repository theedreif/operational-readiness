# Provenance Standard

The Evidence Locker distinguishes the relationship between Ed Reif and every example it contains.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Employed engagement** | Work performed as an employee or contractor, in role |
| **Client engagement** | Work performed under contract with a named client |
| **Independent analysis** | Work written on Ed Reif's own initiative from public information |
| **Speculative proposal** | Work prepared for a specific opportunity but not represented as adopted work |

The purpose of this standard is simple: **separate evidence from implication**.

Case studies should state what the work demonstrates, what Ed Reif's relationship to the work was, and any material limits on what can be publicly shown.

Organisations, platforms, products and standards are named descriptively where relevant. Their names and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.
