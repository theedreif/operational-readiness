# Operational Readiness Doctrine

This directory develops the principles behind field-ready capability.

## Doctrine

1. **Scenario Is the Product** — build around decisions and consequences.
2. **Friction Is the Mechanism** — introduce realistic constraints, ambiguity, and pressure.
3. **Telemetry Over Testing** — capture evidence from behavior and decisions.
4. **Trust Calibration** — prepare people to use automation without blind reliance or reflexive distrust.
5. **Evidence Beats Completion** — demonstrated performance is the meaningful output.
6. **45-Second Rule** — critical guidance must survive field conditions and constrained attention.

These principles shift the design target from *content delivered* to *capability demonstrated*.
